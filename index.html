Colorless and Odorless

DHMO is a colorless and odorless substance, so you may not even know it's coming through your tap.

Extremely Corrosive

DHMO is extremely corrosive to various metals, causing them to rapidly oxidize or even causing explosive reactions.

Dangerous at High Doses

Ingestion of large amounts of DHMO can be dangerous because it can disrupt ion balances and cause hyponatremia.
